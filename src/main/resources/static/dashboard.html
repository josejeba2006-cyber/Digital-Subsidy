APPLICATION PROCESS

How to Receive the Subsidy

The complete process from scheme selection to final installment.

The Digital Subsidy platform follows a structured verification and disbursement workflow. Each stage must be completed before the application can proceed to the next stage.

1

Select Scheme

Browse available government schemes and select a scheme that matches your requirements.

2

Check Eligibility

Your profile details are evaluated against the scheme eligibility criteria.

3

Submit Application

Submit the application along with the required supporting documents.

4

Field Verification

A field officer verifies the submitted application and supporting information.

5

District Review

The district officer reviews the verified application before it moves forward.

6

Bank Verification

Submit your bank details. The finance officer verifies the account before disbursement.

7

Approval

After successful verification, the application is approved and the installment schedule is created.

8

Receive Installments

Subsidy funds are released in stages based on completion of compliance milestones.

BENEFICIARY GUIDELINES

Your Responsibilities

📄 Application & Documents

  • Provide accurate information during application.
  • Upload the required supporting documents.
  • Ensure submitted documents are clear and valid.

🏦 Bank Details

  • Provide correct account holder information.
  • Enter the correct account number and IFSC code.
  • Resubmit corrected details if rejected.

📅 Compliance

  • Complete milestones within the specified due date.
  • Submit utilization proof when required.
  • Respond promptly to rejected proof requests.

💰 Disbursement

  • Installments are released in stages.
  • Subsequent installments depend on milestone completion.
  • Monitor your application and payment status regularly.
